# Prototyping (By an FDE)
> Prototype connects  Expected vs Being Built . For a product or an experience.

 As AI made building cheap, now product management has become the bottleneck. The first thing in product management is product discovery. Why would discovery of requirements be harder than it seems ? Since a user or a set of stakeholders can't provide specification of expected product ( at least most of the time ), however they can explain their problem , talk about what would solve their problem. Thus a complete picture could be drawn if the user has been approached with right tools. Then the product manager decides how to build it with the engineering team. 

 A Forward Deployed Engineer ( is equal to half Software Engineer and half Product manager ), uses Prototype as powerful tool for the entire life-cycle. Each prototype is a question to user, and their reaction is the answer. Moreover it helps the builder to make a blue print while understanding the problem, expected form factor and limitations in building it.

This is the first in a short series on Chapter 1 of This Is a Prototype by Scott Witthoft (Stanford d.school). Next: what a prototype is, and what it isn’t.
