## This is a ProtoType
My reflections on "This is prototype" by  SCOTT WITTHOFT from d.school.


Prototyping would have given me lot more mileage in my journey , both in career and in product building . I have tried building product for  B2C ( A market place to gather Retail investors and Fund managers )   
For my B2C  I had been building a full-stack app both back end and Mobile app for 6 months ( before the era of ChatGPT ) and finally saw how difficult to start marketing and already ran out of run way both energy and savings. Instead if I built the marketing material , awareness , various interactions with potential users could have been a game changer. Suppose if no interactions with potential users had happened yet , I would come to know I had to pivot my idea.  

I could have built a Functional prototype quicker( which is combination of Looks Like and also shows how would it work or works like ). The back end needn't be production ready but shows a real use case clearly.  Thus I could have explored how is it received by users, how can I differentiate my offering from other offerings by fixing short falls from similar offerings. Thus I can validate the new offering with my potential customer.  I could have built this with "Nothing more than needed" i,e. with no backend but entire simulation built on front end with an react app on browser.       I could have brought users who signed up through mailing list on my landing page for an experience of their specific case.

I could listed my curiosities like , and catalog them,

## Functional 

   - Who is my user/customer ?
   - Why do they need it ? 
   - Is there real benefit out of this app, could they save time, hurdle or money through this ?

## Cognitive

- Are they willing to pay for it ?
- How hard to adopt this new platform ? 
- What is the learning curve ? is it worth the effort for the new user ? 

## Emotional 
- How can they trust such an app ? 
- What is the potential risk that they would encounter ? 
- Would they suggest this to other friends ? 


Test your ideas the most effectively, realistically economically before you start building it.
